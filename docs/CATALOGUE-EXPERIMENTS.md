## Decisions over time

- Days 1–14: validate delivery, media clarity, links and profile-to-store path; inspect saves/shares and real visits. No promised minimum views or sales. New music format publication must be checked after its due time.
- Day 30: compare products/angles with enough exposure, label sparse samples, prepare fresh images for promising and underexplained products. Do not rank only by raw reach across Reels and carousels.
- Day 60: replace or refresh the prepared batch using actual customer questions, clicks, carts and fulfilled-order economics. The evergreen reserve prevents an abrupt publishing stop while new content is prepared.
- Day 90: retain commercially useful formats, improve product landing messages where evidence supports it, and consider a separate affordable-loss paid test only after explicit spending approval.

## Measurement and learning limits

The cloud publisher automatically collects available Buffer post metrics and maintains per-destination IDs/status. Null, unavailable or default-zero metrics are not evidence of zero engagement. Facebook uses product-specific UTM links; Instagram's existing bio link limits exact post attribution. Shopify remains the source of real orders; simulated orders are excluded.

The weekly Codex review can privately reconcile Shopify visits/carts/orders/contribution with social patterns and change future organic copy/briefs. That review needs Codex/computer availability and store access; the cloud publisher does not. No external Shopify analytics credential or automatic profit optimizer has been invented. No evidence yet establishes a winning product, ideal posting hour, guaranteed discovery, or a conversion forecast.

## Music and disclosure

These are deliberately designed product-photo slideshows, not fabricated real customer footage. The ambient composition is original synthesis, not a sample, celebrity song, realistic generated voice or simulation of a product's onboard audio. Native Instagram photo-post songs require manual completion through Buffer; automatic music is therefore provided on Reels. Optional image AI wording/self-disclosure is off at the user's request; mandatory future disclosures and platform-added labels remain subject to Meta's rules.

Two new lifestyle photographs were generated using the exact PageGlow and DawnRise source images. Other catalogue images are the existing store photographs placed intact into native layouts. Product features come from the current Shopify descriptions as requested. Pricing, shipping and promotions are unchanged; offer content excludes S3 from the complimentary StillNight promotion and does not combine it with SOLACEN10.
