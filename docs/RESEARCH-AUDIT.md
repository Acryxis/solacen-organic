## Documented platform behaviour

Meta describes personalized recommendations to people beyond an account's followers and emphasizes original Facebook content. This supports producing original, relevant product compositions. It does not establish that static images always outperform Reels, that daily posts will be recommended, or that AI visuals alone will sell.

Buffer's official documentation confirms free-plan API access and ten simultaneously scheduled posts per channel. The rolling cloud refill addresses that concrete queue limit. Its live API accepted S5 images, automatic scheduling, Instagram AI disclosure and Facebook feed posts; actual first posts were confirmed sent on both platforms.

## Corrections to the report

- S5 is an eye massager. Neck/shoulder-treatment examples in the supplied report are not used as S5 claims.
- Feed captions on Instagram are not assumed to provide clickable product URLs. Facebook carries individual UTMs; Instagram's existing profile link gives coarser attribution.
- Daily AI-photo API prices in the report are not a spending quote. The implemented system uses included batch generation and retrieval with no paid model API.
- The suggested weighted profit score mixes currencies, counts and rates without normalization. It is not implemented as a mathematical optimizer. Real orders/contribution lead decisions, with sparse samples labelled uncertain.
- Ninety daily posts do not guarantee enough traffic or orders to identify a statistically reliable winner.
- Organic means no purchased media placement; it does not mean a zero-cost business or passive fulfilment.

## Controlled initial hypotheses

1. A recognisable S5 product and specific features generate more qualified interest than generic calming quotes.
2. A colour/feature carousel can answer more questions than a single image. Compare downstream intent; delivery is not randomized.
3. Direct Facebook links and explanatory captions can generate visits despite modest reach.
4. A calm premium setting can attract the intended evening-routine audience without medical claims.
5. Clear gift instructions can reduce confusion between S5 technology and the separate StillNight sleep mask.

Review at days 14 and 30. Change one useful variable where possible, and do not declare a winner solely because it received more impressions or likes.
